Seven laws from the references

  1. Tempo lane 81–100 BPM, median 93.4. Kendrick's 134 is a 67 BPM half-time feel.
  2. The low end owns the record: median 74% of energy at or below 150 Hz. The 250 Hz–2 kHz band stays thin for the rapper.
  3. One 2-bar loop runs 60–120 bars. Arrangement happens by taking things away.
  4. Single-bar drop-outs mark sections. The MC enters alone and the drums return on bar 2.
  5. Swing lives in the hats: Premier 69–74%, West Coast 57–61%, In Da Club bounce 66%.
  6. Narrow image: median side−mid −19 dB. 90s Wu-Tang and Nas files are effectively mono.
  7. Loud but breathing: modern masters −8 to −10 LUFS with 10.5–13 dB crest.
